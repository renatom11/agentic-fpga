One project, start to finish

This page follows one FPGA project end to end: uart_lite, a fixed-rate 115200 baud 8N1 serial port for a 50 MHz board, five modules, built by AI agents under one human sponsor. Thirty-nine beats, from an empty repository to a signed-off receiver. Press play, or drag the scrubber. Click any box in the repository to see what is inside it at that point on the timeline.

uart_lite is a deliberately small example, invented for this page. Nothing here was synthesised or placed; the LUT and flip-flop figures are budgets a synthesis report has to discharge, not measurements. The way the work is organised, including the refusals, the bounces, the sealed predictions and the audit findings, is drawn from the real programme the rest of this site documents, where this arc took months rather than minutes.
