FIRST PRINCIPLES OPINION — docs/PROCESS.md, third edition

I read the document (all major sections; opening, §3.3–3.5, §6 whole, Annexes A/B in full) and verified the load-bearing citations below against the file. My lens: strip the question to what is actually being solved. The owner wants an organization that can be re-adopted after export attempts failed. Judged from the ground up, most of the council's energy is pointed at the wrong half of the problem, and Sal's framework — excellent as an instrument — is aimed at a claim the document no longer makes.

1. THE COUNCIL IS GRADING A RETRACTED CLAIM. Sal's §0 states the object's claim as "sufficient to hand to a stranger or a blank AI to replicate the organization," and makes C5 (blank AI + this document + empty repo) the master test. But the third edition explicitly retracted that claim: §6.0 opens "This document is half of an export unit, and it is the half that explains rather than the half that runs," and reports the executed measurement that settles the matter — a cold adopter working from the text alone reconstructed the executable layer as "roughly one hundred percent invention" (§6.0, confirmed by the committed halt log at docs/reports/process-council/round-2/). Charlie's F9 saw this; I push it to its conclusion: running C5 against the document alone tests the second edition's claim, and every "blocked leg" it returns against the prose half (Bill F2, F3; Charlie F6, F10) is partly an artifact of the mis-aimed test. Per Sal's own three grades, PROCESS.md alone is description-grade BY DESIGN, and the design decision is correct — §6.3's two-sided evidence ("rule sets extracted from working programs do not transplant" AND "reasons did not regenerate mechanisms even for the people who owned them") is the best first-principles argument in the whole corpus. The verdict must name its object: grade the export unit, or grade the half and say so. Grading the half against the unit's claim manufactures counterfeit CRITICALs.

2. THE REAL SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE IS THE UNPINNED SHELL, AND THE EFFORT INVERSION AROUND IT. Rebuild the problem from zero: what replicates an organization is executable machinery plus the rationale to adapt it. The adoption run's 18 halts are the decisive dataset, and read as one pattern: every halt is a BINDING failure — a literal never stated, a token unbound, a form unshaped, a repository unnamed — and none is a rationale failure. The document's comparative advantage is rationale; its every empirical failure is where it binds or refuses to bind. The document drew the right conclusion ("The shell is the normative source of every grammar," Read this first) and then failed to follow it: the prose half carries ~135 posture stamps, an edition anchor, and a boundary confession, while the executable half — the one the document itself says is primary — carries NO anchor at all: no SHA, no tag, no manifest, and the doc–shell drift check "does not exist yet" (§6.0 [PLANNED]; owed per Annex B.2.9). Bill's F1 is the council's most important finding and I would raise it to CRITICAL without the "borderline": maximal audit of the half that cannot replicate, zero audit anchoring of the half that does, is an inversion of the whole apparatus's purpose. Two adopters cloning on different dates inherit different machinery under one document — C5.8's silent-divergence class, at the unit's foundation. Acceptance test: Sal's C10.2 edition-anchoring probe, run against the shell.

3. THE DOCUMENT'S OWN LAWS GUARANTEE IT GETS WORSE AT ITS JOB. Structural point, not a style point. The margin rule forbids deleting superseded text ("the running line carries the current truth and the margin carries the history"); every correction round therefore adds mass; the boundary admission concedes the stamps cover a 1,445-line predecessor inside a 4,142-line present with "no visible seam"; and the fork contract (§6.1) then instructs the adopter to strip stamps, margins, [CORRECTED] narratives, and Annex B — i.e., the document formally concedes that the majority of its mass does not transfer, and assigns the strip to the party holding the least context (Bill F6, the sharpest structural catch in the three reports). A reference whose transferable fraction shrinks monotonically under its own maintenance rules is the wrong shape of artifact. What this actually is: two artifacts fused — (a) an adopter-facing rationale-and-form reference, and (b) the origin program's epistemic audit trail (stamps, margins, C-nn citations, Annex B — every one program-local by its own label). The org already keeps (b) superbly in its record: the posture list, the halt log, the council rounds, the ADRs. The cure is the document's own fork contract EXECUTED ONCE BY THE EXPORTER: ship a stripped, rule-first adopter's edition as a fourteenth kit-table row, and let the third edition remain the origin's internal record. Acceptance test already exists in the text: Annex B.1's standing cold-reader row ("edition 3: owed"), run against the stripped edition. Note the symmetry: the exporter-not-adopter argument the document itself makes for the drift check ("asking the party with the least information to perform the verification the party with the most information skipped is not a residue, it is a shirk," §6.0) applies verbatim to the strip, and the document does not notice.

4. ONE TEXTUAL FINDING SURVIVES EVEN AFTER RE-AIMING THE QUESTION: CHARLIE'S F1. I verified the collision: line 1654's packet table has the sealed prediction authored by "Whichever seat will be scored"; §3.9 (line 2312) has the auditor authoring manifests with results "scored against predictions sealed before any defect existed"; the §3.3 facsimile (line 1950) says "before any evidence existed" and (lines 1989–90) "scored by the reviewing seat named in the commissioning packet — never by the party whose work the evidence grades." Author, freeze point, and scorer of the flagship mechanism's central artifact are each stated at least twice, differently. This is the one place in the text where two faithful replicators diverge SILENTLY at an independence boundary — the exact class the two non-negotiables exist to prevent — and per Sal's own scoring note it caps C5 regardless of everything else. Top textual blocker; Charlie's acceptance test is right.

5. BOB'S F1 IS THE ROOT CATEGORY ERROR MADE VISIBLE. A description that enacts a packet-minting closure rule existing nowhere in the constitution, unstamped, is the map legislating over the territory — and confusion about where normativity lives is, from first principles, the reason exports fail at all. The org's canonical-statement rule (PROTOCOL §11) exists precisely because restatements rot into rivals; PROCESS.md is the largest restatement in the repo and Bob caught it crossing the line once. One instance, but the council should treat the class, not the sentence: the precedence declaration Sal's C10.1 demands (constitution and scripts win on conflict) is the cheap fix that makes the class harmless.

6. WHAT THE VERDICT'S BLOCKING LIST SHOULD BE: ACTS, NOT PROSE. The successful instrument in this record is not the document — it is the LOOP: cold adopter run, halt log, repairs keyed to halts, standing cold-reader row. The document discovered this itself ("An adoption procedure that has never been executed is a hypothesis"). So the council should resist the gravitational pull of most proposed cures — more facsimiles, more forms, inlined charter fields, subsection prose — because each adds mass to the half that does not transfer and deepens the spiral of point 3. Where Bill F3/F4 and Charlie F2/F6 found missing forms and literals, the document's own placement rule routes the cure to the shell's kit, with one-line pointers here; the council should say so explicitly rather than accept doc-side patches. My blocking list, in order: (i) pin the shell and build the drift check (Bill F1 / Annex B.2.9 — already owed, by the exporter, in the document's own words); (ii) close the seal ambiguity (Charlie F1); (iii) execute the fork contract once, exporter-side, producing the adopter's edition, and run B.1's cold-reader row against it (Bill F6/F5, Charlie F3/F4/F5 all collapse into this single act); (iv) declare precedence (Bob F1's class). Cheap non-blockers worth doing: the two-column name map (Bill F7) and a mechanically derived ToC — both are navigation, not norm, so they cost no drift.

Final position on the headline: description-grade for the document alone, by its own honest construction — and the council should say that this is not the failure it sounds like. The failure would be shipping the unit with its executable half unpinned, its binding instrument unbuilt, and its only transferable rendering left as an exercise for the reader. The document has already written down everything needed to fix itself; the review's job is to make its owner follow the document's own reasoning to its structural conclusion.
