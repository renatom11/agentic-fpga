BOB — AUDIT OF docs/PROCESS.md (third edition, 2026-08-12) AGAINST THE RECORD

VERDICT SUMMARY. The third edition is, by a wide margin, the most accurate edition of this document the program has produced: I re-executed its headline figures against the machinery and the record and nearly all of them hold (details in the sampling disclosure at the end). The findings below are therefore fewer and smaller than the fifteen-FALSE harvest of the posture round — but they exist, several are of exactly the classes this edition claims to have purged (unqualified quantifiers, header-counts contradicting their lists, figures quoted from a source instead of re-derived, aspiration stated as rule), and one is a genuinely new pattern: a norm enacted by writing it down, unstamped, in the document that elsewhere refuses to do precisely that. Findings ranked by severity; each carries its criterion under Sal's framework and names the contradicting artifact.

[F1] MAJOR — C8 (enforcement honesty), cross-ref C2 — §3 "The taxonomy is closed by a rule, not by a list": the closure rule exists nowhere but in this document, carries no posture, and the record practices the opposite.
The blockquote — "A new packet type may be minted only by the amendment procedure (§2.7): a numbered decision record naming the type, its required fields, its relay class and its state vocabulary, accepted by a seat that did not propose it. No seat mints a type by using one." — is presented as "The rule:" and "the invariant worth exporting." Verified against the record: agents/PROTOCOL.md §3 defines four packet types (WO/SO/BUG/RV) and contains no minting rule, no relay-class-at-minting duty, no state-vocabulary duty; no ADR in docs/adr/ (0001–0021) mints one; no charter carries one. The record's actual practice is the inverse of the rule: every extension type was minted by use — HT-01_first-harvest-transit.md, WO-0063B_pre-run-reading-note.md, WO-0046_cosim-phase-1-adjudication.md, the campaign packet/manifest/brief family — and the document itself concedes this two paragraphs later ("None of these was minted by the route above"). Three aggravators. (a) The rule carries neither a C-nn stamp nor the "(no posture row — added after the measurement; posture: …, carried by …)" marker that every other normative addition in this edition scrupulously carries — by the document's own stamp grammar ("Anything without a row is unmeasured… It states its posture in prose because that is the strongest thing available to it"), this rule states no posture at all, and there is no carrier to state. (b) It is not routed as an amendment candidate in Annex B.2, although the same edition routed §2.7's fourth requirement with the explicit ground "A document that discovers a rule its own constitution should carry proposes it; it does not enact it by writing it down" — this blockquote enacts by writing down. (c) The table beneath it says "Six types are defined": the constitution defines four; the sealed prediction and the finding are packet types only in this document (PROTOCOL §10 defines the seal as an artifact with no relay class; findings' verbatim status comes from the E4/relay clause, not from a packet taxonomy) — the "defined" list matches neither the constitution (4) nor the practice (4 + seals + findings + 5 unrouted extensions), and nothing in the table says which rows are constitutional and which are this document's construction. Consequence if left: a replicator inherits, as this org's standing law, a closure rule the source org neither states in any binding artifact nor has ever obeyed — the exact aspiration-described-as-reality failure the posture apparatus was built to prevent, in the one place the apparatus was not applied. (Partial mitigation, stated for fairness: for types that live in constitutional text, the rule is derivable from PROTOCOL §11; the unrouted-extension admission is honest. The defect is the unmarked posture and the false "defined," not the desirability of the rule.)

[F2] MAJOR — C2 (accuracy), cross-ref C9 — the shared-scope arithmetic is wrong twice, contradicts the document's own enumeration, and was quoted from the source instead of re-derived — in the passage that commands "Compute it."
§2.3 states twice: "Four path classes are shared by two to four seats" (lines 1267–1269 and 1288–1289). The document's own §2.1, two sections earlier, enumerates the packet directory as "granted to three leads and all four worker roles" — seven seats. scripts/policy.sh (lines 173–213) makes it seven to eight: agents/handoffs/* is writable by architect_docs_lead, rtl_lead, rtl_lead_md, dv_lead, rtl_module_dev, tb_writer, data_wrangler, formal_dv. The product tree is not "a lead and its worker" but three seats (rtl_lead, rtl_lead_md, rtl_module_dev — §2.1's enumeration omits the contingent seat entirely, though the §2.3 facsimile includes its row). The "two to four" figure descends verbatim from posture row C-40's evidence cell ("agents/handoffs/*, libs/*, test/*, tools/* are each shared by 2–4 seats" — itself wrong for handoffs), i.e., the document quoted its source's number rather than re-deriving it — violating its own §3.9 instruction ("Import the rules; re-derive the numbers") and §4.3's receiving-seat rule, inside the very passage that says "Read the arithmetic off it before you trust it… Compute it: for every pair of seats, intersect their scopes." Related facsimile infidelity (C2.3 grounds, MAJOR-minimum per Sal): the scope-table facsimile's contingent-seat cell reads "as its line's lead, scoped to the sub-domain it activates for" — policy.sh grants rtl_lead_md the full rtl_lead scope with no sub-domain narrowing anywhere in the machine layer; the narrowing lives only in charter prose, i.e., it is review-enforced, and the facsimile presents it as table content. Consequence: an adopter copies a scope model whose overlap census is understated by a factor of two on its widest class, and inherits a machine-checked-looking narrowing that is actually convention.
