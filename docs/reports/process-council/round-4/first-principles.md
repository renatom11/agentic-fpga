FIRST PRINCIPLES OPINION — the council is grading the wrong object, and so is the owner.

**1. The claim under review no longer exists in the document — and the place it moved to is empty.** Sal's framework grades "complete, project-agnostic, blank-AI-replicable." The fifth edition withdrew that claim on page one: "The replication claim belongs to the unit, not to this half" (preamble; §6.0). So the real question is not "is this description fit," it is "does the unit exist." Strip assumptions and check: (a) both adoption runs ran in worlds that could not reach the shell — §6.2 says so in bold: "nothing about the machine half was verifiable from that world"; (b) the shell half is domain-bound — I fetched it myself, its README's first line is "A reusable AI-agent organization **for FPGA programs**," while §6.0 calls it "extracted project-free" (BILL-01 confirmed; the document's one materially false load-bearing sentence); (c) the instrument that would bind the halves — the doc–shell drift check — "does not exist," confessed for the third consecutive edition (B.10·2, B.2·9). The replication claim has been relocated to a composite artifact that has never once been assembled and tested as a composite. Every finding the three auditors filed is real, but they are findings about the tested half of an untested whole.

**2. The organization has exempted its flagship export from its own founding principle.** This org's deepest law is §2.4 (mechanical refusal over advisory warning) and §5.1 ("remedies decay without mechanical checks"). PROCESS.md is a 5,446-line remedy with no mechanical check: the drift check is exactly what happens when you apply the org's own first principle to its own description, and it is "the oldest unpaid item in this annex." Worse, the org outlawed reading a scoreboard in place of a suite (§3.8/§3.9, post-ADR-0020: "no ratio stands in for the dispositions") — yet the export claim's headline evidence is precisely a scoreboard: "18 halts/3 stops → 17 halts/0 stops," measured on the fallback path (document-only) rather than the path a real adopter takes (shell in hand, facing §6.2 versus the shell's own BOOTSTRAP.md — two orders, no tiebreaker, never executed; BILL-02). The gate has never asked the suite, because the unit has no suite. For a program this scrupulous about sealed predictions and external anchors, the export is its one unanchored oracle.

**3. Diagnose the defect profile by its selection pressure.** Five editions of this document were shaped by council audits, not by adopters. You get the phenotype you select for, and all three reports independently confirm it: unimpeachable micro-claims (Bob could not manufacture a single false [MC] across 14 probes — genuinely exceptional), unusable bulk (B− readability, core/memoir separation refused twice, "the endorsement is now overdue" — Charlie F1, BILL-06), and potholes confessed rather than repaired (five known order defects budgeted at "about an hour each" — per adopter, forever — BILL-03). The confession apparatus, invented to kill false confidence, has acquired a second function: it lets defects ship indefinitely at a fixed cost to the writer and a recurring cost to every reader. That inverts the objective function. A document whose purpose is re-adoption must minimize adopter cost; this one minimizes exporter dishonesty. Those are different maxima, and §5.5's own debt law (named owner + closing event) is enforced for small debts and rolled for the three that decide the owner's goal: the drift check, the stripped edition, the with-shell run.

**4. Direct answer to the council's framing question — is a process reference the right artifact?** Yes, as a component; no, as this artifact. The document's own §6.3 is the correct first-principles analysis: rules without reasons read as ceremony and get dropped where they cost most; and "reasons did not regenerate mechanisms even for the people who owned them" (21 ADRs, 51 self-test cases to get from lived incidents to working scripts). So reasons must ship in prose and machinery must ship as code — the two-half bet is right. But the current file is three artifacts fused: an adopter's handbook (the ~quarter that transfers: §1–§5's rules, failure classes, facsimiles — run 2 proved these now move intact), the origin's self-governance memoir (margins, 50 sentinels, ~135 stamps, 670 lines of Annex B — valuable, and belongs at home), and an audit ledger. The document already discovered this about itself in its most honest sentence: "it fails exactly where it impersonates the machine half" (§6.0) — and then refused the surgery twice (B.9 refusal 1, B.10 refusal 1). The owner's previous exports failed for the reason §6.3 states; this export will fail for a different one: the half that runs is domain-loaded and untested as shipped, while the half that explains has been polished past the point of marginal return. Run 2 is the proof: with a near-perfect document, the adopter still wrote 850 lines of enforcement from nothing. The constraint is not, and has not been for two editions, the prose.

**5. What I would have the council commission — not a sixth edition.** (a) Build the drift check; it is already assigned to the exporter's seat and it converts "complete description of an amendable machine" from a claim that is guaranteed to go silently false into a checkable property — without it every facsimile is testimony. (b) De-domain the shell or relabel it honestly, with a file-by-file inventory of domain load versus mechanism (BILL-01's repair) — the shell, not the document, is the replication vehicle. (c) Execute the fork contract once, exporter-side, producing the stripped rule-first adopter's edition the council already prescribed — this simultaneously discharges Charlie F1, BILL-06, and most of C3. (d) Then run the only acceptance test that grades the actual claim: one cold adoption, alien domain, shell reachable, both orders on the table — as the unit's dated condition. Until (d) runs, the honest verdict available to any council on the owner's question is not FIT or NOT FIT but UNTESTED-AS-SHIPPED. (e) Ride-along repairs regardless of the above, because they touch the human gate on the export path itself: the sponsor guide missing the sponsor's fifth power (Bob F1 — the holder of the harvest-refusal power reads a "complete" list without it) and the unrouted false constitutional clause (Bob F2), plus the countersignature's missing physical form (Charlie F4) — the one constitutive mechanism nobody could generate from the text.

One sentence for the record: this program built the most honest description of an agent organization I have seen, and honesty was the wrong remaining investment — the fifth edition spent its round perfecting the confession of debts whose repair was cheaper than the confession, and the council should now score the unit, refuse further document rounds as out of scope, and put the next round's budget into the shell, the drift check, and the one experiment that has never been run.
