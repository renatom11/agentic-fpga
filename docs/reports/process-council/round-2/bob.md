F1 — MAJOR (C2 Accuracy; veto-class). A false limb about the amendment machinery, inside the edition whose premise is that all false claims were purged. §2.7: "The record shows the procedure working, twice, and shows its debt: an instrument in force with its constitutional diffs and its owed test cases still unapplied." The constitutional-diffs half is true (PROTOCOL §4–§5 still describe single-file journals and R1–R9; no R10/R11 text). The test-case half is false: ADR-0017 §9's eight owed cases landed 2026-08-03 at commit a0454b4 ("WO-0053: journal volume chain enforced — R10 live, R11 minted, verifier shipped") and exist as S29–S37 in scripts/test_protocol.sh, running green in CI inside the very 51-case count §6.3 itself quotes ("a self-test grown to fifty-one cases" — verified by execution: "protocol self-test: 51 passed"). The document cannot simultaneously count those cases in §6.3 and call them unapplied in §2.7 (also a C9 internal contradiction). This is exactly the defect class the edition exists to remove, in the section describing the procedure that removes it.

F2 — MAJOR (C1 Comprehensiveness). The DV-escape ledger is entirely absent. PROTOCOL §10: "The auditor owns the DV-escape ledger (docs/reports/audit/): any post-sign-off divergence found later is recorded there, not by DV." auditor charter §3: "DV-escape ledger (mandatory): you own docs/reports/audit/dv_escapes.md — every post-sign-off divergence... recorded there by you (not by DV)". Zero mentions in PROCESS.md (grep "escape": none). This is a named, chartered, mandatory independence mechanism of precisely the document's own root class (§5.7 — the seat whose miss is being counted must not own the count of its misses), and §3.8's own failure-class prose ("distinguish 'we fixed it' from 'we never had the problem'") is what the ledger serves. An adopter builds a replica that certifies sign-offs and has no accounting for what those sign-offs later missed. Silent omission — reads as simplicity, which is the failure mode Sal's C1 names.
