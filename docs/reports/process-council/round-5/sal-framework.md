# EVALUATION FRAMEWORK — docs/PROCESS.md (sixth edition) and its companion volume

Issued by SAL, independent audit agent, 2026-08-12. For three reviewers and an adjudicating council. This is a working instrument: run the probes, file findings in the prescribed form, score the vector, hand the council the conflicts. It was calibrated against the live repository (constitution, scripts, journals, charters, packets, ADRs, audit artifacts) so that criteria test the document against reality, not taste. Ground-truth facts reviewers may reuse without re-deriving are collected in Part V.

---

## PART I — WHAT THE DOCUMENT MUST ACHIEVE (the job description this instrument scores against)

The document class is rare and demanding: a **self-describing replication kit for a governed multi-agent organization**. It must simultaneously be:

1. **A faithful record** of a real, running system — the constitution (`agents/PROTOCOL.md`), the enforcement scripts, the journal chains, the packet traffic, the gates, the audit campaigns — such that a claim in the document can be checked against a file in the repository.
2. **A teaching text** — a human (sponsor, adopter, future reviewer) must be able to understand why each rule exists and which rules are load-bearing.
3. **An executable-enough specification** — its own central claim is that it plus a named shell repository form an "export unit" from which a blank AI in an empty repository can rebuild the organization for a different project.

These three jobs pull against each other, and the document knows it (it declares a facsimile rule, a posture-stamp apparatus, a core/memoir split, an adoption order tested by two committed halt logs). The framework therefore scores not only whether each job is done, but whether the tensions were resolved deliberately and honestly. Two structural facts frame everything: (a) the document's evidentiary spine (its `C-nn` posture stamps) was measured when the file was 1,445 lines; it is now 4,920, and it discloses that most of its text is unmeasured; (b) the sixth edition split history into `docs/PROCESS-MEMOIR.md`, which states no rule and yields to the core wherever they disagree.

## PART II — HOW TO RUN THE REVIEW

**Reviewer seats** (assign one each; a reviewer may run another seat's probe only after completing their own):
- **R1 — Record-checker.** Full repository access. Owns C1, C2, C6, C8; supports C7, C10. Works by re-execution: greps, diffs, script reads, path stats.
- **R2 — Cold human reader.** MUST NOT open the repository (beyond the two volumes) until their first-pass probes are logged; their ignorance is the instrument. Owns C3, C7; supports C9, C10, C5.7.
- **R3 — Executor / blank-AI proxy.** Drives a fresh agent session with controlled context. Owns C4, C5; supports C9.

**Finding form** (every finding, no exceptions):
`{criterion id; doc anchor (section + line); the claim, quoted; probe run (command or protocol); observed; expected; severity CRITICAL/MAJOR/MINOR; verdict CONFIRMED (re-executed) or PLAUSIBLE (read-only)}`.
- CRITICAL: an adopter following this text would build a broken or dishonest control, or the document's central claim is materially false.
- MAJOR: a load-bearing mechanism is missing, wrong, or unfindable; recoverable by a competent reader at real cost.
- MINOR: friction, blemish, drift with no execution consequence.

**Sampling discipline** (the framework obeys the law it tests): every sampled probe reports population, sample size, and the result distribution — never a bare ratio. "18 of 20 stamps confirmed" with the two failures named, not "90%".

**Scoring**: each criterion scored 0–4. 4 = all probes pass; residuals MINOR. 3 = MAJOR defects exist but every one is disclosed by the document itself. 2 = at least one undisclosed MAJOR. 1 = fails on a load-bearing point; the text misleads. 0 = the criterion is untestable because the document lacks the apparatus to test it. Report the 10-score vector; never average (Part IV rule 8).

**Self-disclosure rule**: a defect the document discloses about itself still scores against its criterion — disclosure caps severity one grade below the undisclosed equivalent, EXCEPT where the disclosure is itself inaccurate (then C7 CRITICAL) or where the disclosed absence guts the central claim (e.g., the admitted nonexistence of the doc–shell drift check stays at least MAJOR). Motto for the whole review: **self-conviction is not immunity, and honesty is not coverage.**

## PART III — THE CRITERIA

### C1 — COMPREHENSIVE DOCUMENTATION
**Statement.** The document covers everything the organization actually is and does: every seat, every artifact class, every enforcement rule, every lifecycle, every exception — or declares the exclusion and points correctly at where the excluded thing lives.
**Why it matters here.** The document claims to be the explaining half of a complete export unit. A silent gap is not a missing paragraph; it is a mechanism the adopter will not build, and the record shows the mechanisms omitted first are the ones invented last and worst (both committed adoption runs stalled exactly on the unstated).
**Probes.**
1.1 *Inventory-vs-coverage matrix.* Take the ground-truth inventory (Part V). For each row, locate the core section that covers it, or the declared exclusion that points at it. Any load-bearing mechanism with neither is MAJOR. Known hard rows to check first: journal-chain rotation and volume headers (scripts enforce an R10 with sha256-linked volumes and soft/hard size thresholds — does §2.2/§2.6 carry all of it?); the R11 blob gate and its journal carve-out; the auditor's transcription exception; foreign journal seeding; spawn short-ids in shared worker journals; packet numbering authority; dispatch-only commissions (ADR-0022 is one); the sealed-predictions sibling files; the Mutation record's full disposition algebra (negative controls, UNSCOREABLE renders, equivalent mutants, unreachable assertions, killing-unit-present-at-gate-SHA); the lessons harvest with LH1–LH3 and the two LH2 grades; the genesis/bootstrap paradox; the rehydration drill.
1.2 *Actor coverage.* For each of the nine seats plus sponsor, council, and worker roles, answer from the document alone: (a) what may it stage, (b) what must it produce per round, (c) who reviews it, (d) how does it sign a gate, (e) what happens when it fails or refuses? A seat with two of five unanswerable is a gap.
1.3 *Lifecycle walkthroughs against the record.* Walk five real lifecycles and check every step reality performed is stated or pointed to: (a) a work order DRAFT→ISSUED→RETURNED→ACCEPTED/BOUNCED including the Return log and multi-round re-issue (compare `agents/handoffs/WO-0038_*`, six rounds); (b) a sign-off FAIL→re-issue→PASS with superseded verdicts preserved unedited (compare `SO-xgmii_rx_64.md`, four rounds); (c) a seeded-defect campaign end to end: sealed manifest → transient application by the orchestrator → suite run → per-class disposition → gate re-check; (d) an amendment: ADR → journal entry → self-test case when enforcement semantics change; (e) rehydration after loss of the orchestrator session.
1.4 *Reverse sweep.* Anything in the document with no counterpart in reality — hand it to C2 as a possible fabrication or to C7 as an unmarked aspiration.
1.5 *Exclusion honesty.* For each declared exclusion (domain nouns; grammar normativity delegated to the shell; history delegated to the memoir): is the pointer correct, and is what remains sufficient to act on? An exclusion whose pointer dangles is a gap wearing a label.
**Scoring note.** Weight by load: a missing paragraph on the sponsor's five-item surface outweighs a missing page on latency reporting.

### C2 — ACCURACY
**Statement.** What the document says is true of the real mechanisms (scripts, CI, constitution) and the real record (journals, packets, ADRs, reports) at its stated anchor commit.
**Why it matters here.** This document teaches adopters to trust specific guarantees ("a violation is refused"). A false enforcement claim is a control that exists only as prose — the exact failure class the document's own §5 museum catalogs. And the document's authority apparatus (posture stamps, anchors, printed falsifiers) invites verification; accuracy is the criterion that accepts the invitation.
**Probes.**
2.1 *Stamp re-execution.* Draw ≥20 posture stamps stratified across §1–§6 and Annex A; re-execute each against the machinery. Examples of the method: an `[MC]` on protected-trailer shadowing → attempt `--extra-trailer "Agent: x"` against `scripts/agent_commit.sh` in a scratch clone and observe the refusal; `[MC · C-45]` chain headers at rotation → read the rotation block of `agent_commit.sh` (Previous-volume, Previous-volume-sha256, Continues-from checks) and construct one violating case. Any `[MC]` that does not refuse: CRITICAL. Any `[RE]` with no named carrier seat/form: MAJOR. Any `[P1]` that never happened or silently recurs as practice: MAJOR.
2.2 *Facsimile-vs-record diff.* The core carries ~10 blocks labeled `FACSIMILE — instance, not norm` (journal entry, trailer block, scope table, rule table, packet skeleton, seal header, countersignature, roster/program-state files, rotated-volume header). Diff each against a real instance: a live journal entry; a real commit's trailers via `git log`; `scripts/policy.sh`'s scope data; the script's actual rule ids (R1–R8, R10 twice, R11); a real WO packet; a real `*-SEALED-predictions.md`; a real RETURNED countersignature block. Structural divergence is a finding; note the document's own precedence rule (shell/reality wins, the facsimile is the defect).
2.3 *Event-claim verification.* The two adoption runs and their halt/stop counts against the committed logs (`docs/reports/process-council/round-2/adoption-run-halt-log.md`, `round-3/adoption-run-2-halt-log.md` — both exist); the fourth council verdict commissioning the split (`round-4/verdict.md` — exists); the "128 claims / 15 false / 8 owed" figures against `docs/reports/audit/PROCESS-claims-posture.md`; the edition anchor's self-measurements (the 4,920-line figure is exact at this state — re-run `wc -l`); the sibling-landing declaration (verify `91f005d` is a descendant of `7b749f0` and touched no path of either volume).
2.4 *Quantity audit.* Re-derive every count the document states over its own record: 35 blocks / 330 lines moved; 752 annex lines; 1,082 total; twelve dialect terms; "thirteen rows, eleven numbers" in the §2.6 rule table; the per-section `· n` citation counts via the printed command.
2.5 *Path-resolution census.* Extract every repository path the core cites; `stat` each. (Pre-verified set in Part V.)
2.6 *Law-vs-record lag.* The repository is itself unevenly updated (e.g., the dv_lead charter still says "mutation kills N/N" while ADR-0020 and the constitution's Mutation record outlaw a ratio standing in for dispositions). The document is accurate if it states current law and does not pretend the record is uniformly conformant; it fails if it presents either the stale text as law or the record as clean.
**Scoring note.** Distinguish false-of-the-machinery (worst), false-of-the-record, and stale-at-anchor (claim was true at the anchor commit and says so — not a finding; the anchor is the contract).

### C3 — HUMAN READABILITY AND CLARITY
**Statement.** A competent human who did not live through the program can read it, navigate it, and come away with correct understanding at acceptable cost.
**Why it matters here.** The sponsor ratifies gates on the strength of understanding this text; an adopter's engineers will read it under pressure; the document's own theory is that a rule whose failure class you understand survives pressure and one you don't gets dropped. Its writing style is dense, heavily bolded, long-sentence, self-referential — the risk axis is real and the program's own graders scored earlier editions B−/C+ on exactly this.
**Probes** (R2, quarantined from the repo).
3.1 *Cold-read term log.* Read §1 and §2 start to finish; log every term or construct that cannot be inferred at the point met. Then test the dialect's own totality claim ("twelve terms, and the list below is the whole of them"): every logged term must be in the dialect, bold-defined at first use, or a finding.
3.2 *Timed retrieval.* Ten operational questions, answered from Contents + subsection index, ≤5 minutes each, citing the section: who may edit a gate checklist; what a worker's bench failure triggers; when a sign-off may say PASS; what the orchestrator does with a verbatim-class packet; what every commit must stage besides the work; who allocates packet numbers; what an equivalent mutant does to the denominator; what the sponsor's whole surface is; what happens when two leads deadlock; how the org recovers from a dead orchestrator session. Log misses and detours.
3.3 *The doubling test.* The document states every mechanism twice — rule and failure class — and claims the failure class makes the rule re-derivable. Sample 10 pairs: cover the rule half, have R2 re-derive it from the failure class; then reverse. Score re-derivation fidelity.
3.4 *Sentence-level load.* Sample 20 paragraphs; count sentences needing more than two reads; name structural causes (nested bold qualifiers, stacked negations, mid-sentence stamp/pointer traffic like `[B.2·9]`).
3.5 *Preamble tax.* The preamble claims to be "skippable if you only need to act." Have R2 run probe 3.2 having read only "Read this first" + Contents; measure degradation. ~590 lines precede §1 — is the toll road honest?
3.6 *Worked-example stress test.* The Mutation record (§3.9 territory) is the densest law in the book. After one reading, R2 writes a correct gate tally for a synthetic case (sealed=7, seeded=5, one negative control, one never-rendered, one campaign survivor). If a careful human cannot, the law is unexecutable by the humans who must countersign it.
**Scoring note.** Do not confuse density with unclarity: score against reader success, not against style preference. A long text obeying its fact-once rule is not a finding; a sentence that defeats two readings is.

### C4 — AI-AGENT READABILITY AND CLARITY
**Statement.** An AI agent can parse, navigate, chunk, retrieve from, and act on the document — including under partial-context conditions (retrieval fragments, windowed reads).
**Why it matters here.** The declared consumer includes AI seats: every agent in a replicated org reads this class of text at spawn, and retrieval systems will serve it in fragments. The split was explicitly justified as a retrieval-safety property ("no fragment of the core can contain a superseded claim"). That is a testable engineering claim, not a slogan.
**Probes** (R3).
4.1 *Fragment integrity.* Draw 15 random ~2,000-token windows from the core; give each to a fresh agent with no other context: "state any rules this fragment establishes, and their enforcement posture." Score windows producing a wrong rule or wrong posture. The split's claim predicts zero superseded-law contamination; verify.
4.2 *Token grammar.* Write a ≤20-line parser for the stamp forms and annex pointers; run it over the whole file; count violations of the legend (the backtick-vs-bracket distinction between pointers and stamps is declared load-bearing — confirm it holds everywhere).
4.3 *Anchor navigation.* Verify every Contents and subsection-index anchor resolves as a markdown anchor; an agent must be able to jump by reference, not scan 4,920 lines.
4.4 *Actor-Trigger-Obligation extraction.* Have a fresh agent extract (actor, trigger, obligation, refusal-consequence) tuples from §2.6 and §4; R1 grades them against the scripts' ground truth. This measures whether rules are stated in extractable imperative shape rather than narrative that only a full-document reader can operationalize.
4.5 *Context budget.* Core (4,920 ln) + memoir (1,640 ln) + constitution + one charter + one packet: state the token arithmetic against realistic agent context windows, and check the document applies its own single-file-threshold discipline (Annex A.3) to itself.
4.6 *Cross-file dereference count.* Running 4.4 with the core only: count tasks that silently require the memoir or the shell. Each such dependency must be flagged in-text by the document's own cross-volume rule; unflagged ones are findings.

### C5 — BLANK-AI REPLICABILITY

## PART IV — WEIGHING CONFLICTS BETWEEN CRITERIA (council guidance)

1. **For statements of law, accuracy beats readability.** A beautifully clear false rule is the worst object this document can contain, because it will be executed. No readability credit offsets a CONFIRMED false `[MC]`.
2. **For statements of evidence, calibration beats accuracy.** A true claim overclaimed (review-enforced dressed as machine-checked) outranks a false claim honestly labeled unmeasured: the first trains the adopter to trust the wrong things; the second trains them to check.
3. **Comprehensiveness vs readability/length** is resolved by the declared-exclusion rule: an absence with a correct pointer is not a gap; a fact fully restated in two places is a maintainability defect even where it "helps" the reader. Do not reward padding; do not penalize length that obeys fact-once.
4. **Human vs AI readability**: prefer findings naming constructions that fail both. Where they genuinely trade off, the tiebreak is the adopter-in-motion test — which reading fails during §6.2 execution, at the moment the adopter is least able to judge? C3 and C4 are instrumental; C5 is the terminal purpose.
5. **Accuracy-of-this-program vs portability**: the document's own pattern is correct — describe reality, mark the portable form (the Annex A pattern). Penalize under C2 if it bends this program's facts toward portability; under C9 if it states portable law without marking the substrate.
6. **Split mechanics vs first-contact needs**: mechanical S1–S4 compliance does not excuse a block an adopter needs on first read landing in the memoir. Score under C8.3/C5 regardless.
7. **Disclosed defects**: severity capped one grade below the undisclosed equivalent — except when the disclosure is itself false (C7 CRITICAL) or the disclosed absence guts the central claim (the missing doc–shell drift check stays ≥MAJOR). Honesty is not coverage.
8. **Aggregation**: report the ten-score vector plus the finding list; never average into one number — a 4 on readability cannot buy back a 1 on accuracy. The council verdict is gate-shaped: ACCEPT / ACCEPT-WITH-CONDITIONS (each condition tied to a numbered finding with an owner) / RETURN (with the defect list, per the program's own BOUNCE form).
9. **Convergence rule** (the program's own council doctrine): where two reviewers independently return the same finding from different probes, the convergence itself is the strongest evidence in the round — promote it, and say in the verdict that it converged.
10. **Precedence between volumes**: current law is judged only against the core. A memoir sentence can generate findings only under C8.
