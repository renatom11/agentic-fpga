THE EXECUTOR — Monday-morning verdict on docs/PROCESS.md (sixth edition)

The only question I care about: an adopter clones two repositories Monday morning — do they have a first step, and does the sequence hold to a running organization? Answer: the first step exists and is genuinely good (Step −1 is the best-executable page in the document), the last two-thirds of the sequence hold (Steps 3–6, the DoD, the founding-declaration mechanism, and §1.7's four-act genesis are all executable from the text as written — I could perform the founding-range declaration from §6.2's paragraph alone), and the document self-checks like nothing else in this class. But between minute one and day two there are five specific places where a literal executor stalls or is routed off the map, and every one of them is fixable with text, not machinery. My points, strongest first:

1. Fastest fix, and it is Bill's, and it is right: extract §6.2's order-independent invariants into a ten-line conformance checklist any order must satisfy — founding range declared as committed data; machinery before adversarial review; CI green once before platform protection; empty bypass list verified by live-fire bounce; constitution authored from your own machinery before Step 4 binds to it; gate fully signed before WO-0001.
